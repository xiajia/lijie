A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before you
get to work.

Clear expectations are part of a useful toolkit.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes. The calculators, reference tables, local job library, widgets, and PDF generation work on the device. Purchasing or restoring Pro requires access to Apple’s purchase services. Saving a backup to a cloud provider may also need connectivity.

Can I mix imperial and metric measurements?

Yes. Length fields have their own units, so you can work with feet, inches, fractions, mm, cm, and m in one calculation. Choose the output units and display precision separately.

Does the app include manufacturer fitting dimensions?

No. Enter takeout dimensions from your verified source, or save your own personal presets with Pro. The formula and unit references are calculation aids; check your drawing, specification, fitting data, and job requirements before fabrication.

What can I calculate in a widget?

Medium widgets support Simple Offset. Large widgets support Simple Offset or Cut Length with button-based numeric and fraction entry. Small widgets display a recent result or status. Rolling Offset and Length & Fraction Math are available in the app. Four slots let you keep independent working setups.

Does backup sync my devices automatically?

No. Export a backup file and import it on the other device. You choose its location in Files, including iCloud Drive if available. The backup includes jobs, revision history, presets, settings, and export snapshots; it excludes PDF files and widget drafts.

Is Pro a subscription? What does Restore recover?

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Restore Purchases recovers the Pro entitlement through Apple, not your job library. To move or recover job data, use a backup file. Purchase entitlements and the local free-PDF allowance are not imported from backups.

Do I need an account or have to enable analytics?

No account is required. Usage analytics is optional and requires both in-app permission and system tracking authorization. Declining it does not block the tools, and you can switch it off in the app’s Privacy & Safety settings.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Pipefitter is built for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app supports English and Spanish, portrait and landscape layouts, and Light, Dark, or System appearance. PDF language can be selected independently.
